flickpick.build#

The build pipeline: MovieLens tables -> catalogue, sparse EASE matrix, embeddings.

build_artifacts is pure (tables in, Artifacts out; the only side effect is running the embedding model). tools.build adds the download and the write.

The CF set is the n_items most-rated films that have an IMDb id; idx orders them by rating count (descending, ties by MovieLens id). EASE is trained on ratings at or above like_threshold (on the 0-100 scale; 70 = 3.5 stars), binarised.
